Good to Know

Storage & Usage Notes

General guidance across our range. Exact details for each product are listed on its product card above.

Gojju & Mango Specials

Store in a cool, dry place and always use a clean spoon. Refrigerate for up to 4 months shelf life; if not refrigerated, consume within 4 days.

Pickles

No refrigeration needed. Store in a cool, dry place, and close the pack airtight after every use with a clean, dry spoon.

Puliyogare Gojju Specials

Store cool & dry. Refrigerate for a shelf life of 2 or more months. Always use a clean, dry spoon to serve.

About Havyaka Swaada

A taste rooted in Malnad tradition

Havyaka Swaada is a traditional Havyaka and Malnad-side homemade food brand, offering unique regional products — mango specials, puliyogare gojju flavours, pickles, drinks, and nourishing mixes.

We prepare every batch with regional ingredients and time-tested family recipes, keeping the taste authentic, clean, and honest — never mass-produced.

"Sāmpradāyika, ārōgyakara, ruchikara" — traditional, wholesome, and full of flavour.

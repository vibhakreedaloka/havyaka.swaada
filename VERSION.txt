Havyaka Swaada Website
Version: 1.2.5
Released: 2026-08-08

Changelog:
- 1.2.5 (2026-08-08): Fixed two visual bugs on product cards. (1) The
  "SIGNATURE" badge was overlapping longer product titles like "Nellikaayi
  Puliyogare Gojju" and "Karibevu Puliyogare Gojju" — increased the title's
  reserved space and slightly reduced the badge size so it never overlaps.
  (2) The "+40g FREE · 20% Extra" badge had low text/background contrast
  (dark brown text on gold) — switched to white text on a deeper gold/bronze
  background for clearer legibility, matching the boldness of the green
  "Save ₹X" badge.
- 1.2.4 (2026-08-08): Added Instagram (@havyaka.swaada) and Facebook links.
  Shown in two places: as new rows in the Contact section (alongside Phone,
  Website, Location), and as compact round icon links in the footer, below
  the brand blurb. Both open in a new tab.
- 1.2.3 (2026-08-08): Restyled the Puliyogare Gojju Specials "+40g FREE ·
  20% Extra" note to match the look of the Gojju & Mango Specials "Save ₹X"
  badge — it's now a highlighted pill (gold, to distinguish it from the
  green savings badge) on its own line below the price, instead of plain
  parenthesised text running inline. Variant rows now stack price and any
  badge vertically for both cases.
- 1.2.2 (2026-08-07): Updated the 500g price for Mandan Gojju and Mango GuLa
  from ₹499 to ₹599. Savings badge recalculates automatically — now shows
  "Save ₹146 · 20%" on the 500g pack (previously ₹246 · 33% at ₹499).
- 1.2.1 (2026-08-07): Savings badge on Gojju & Mango Specials now also shows
  the percentage saved alongside the rupee amount, e.g. "Save ₹49 · 16%"
  (percentage is savings ÷ the 100g-equivalent price).
- 1.2.0 (2026-08-07): Corrected Mango GuLa's largest pack size from 600g to
  500g (price unchanged at ₹499). Added a "Save ₹X" badge on Gojju & Mango
  Specials (Mandan Gojju, Mango GuLa) showing the savings on 200g and 500g
  packs versus buying that quantity at the 100g rate. Reformatted Puliyogare
  Gojju Specials pack labels — "FREE" now appears in full caps inside the
  parentheses alongside the extra-quantity note (e.g. "200g ₹299 (+40g FREE
  · 20% Extra)") instead of running into the plain pack label. NutriPower Mix
  savings badges are not yet added — pending real gram quantities per size
  from the brand owner to avoid showing an invented/inaccurate comparison.
- 1.1.1 (2026-08-07): Fixed a bug in the ordering flow — pack sizes/variants
  (e.g. Shot / Medium / Full, or 100g / 200g / 500g) previously behaved as a
  single choice sharing one quantity. Each size now has its own independent
  quantity stepper, so e.g. 1 Shot + 2 Medium + 1 Full of the same product
  can all be added to the order at once. Each is sent as its own line in the
  WhatsApp order message.
- 1.1.0 (2026-08-07): Reworked Products section — all 5 categories now render
  as one continuous scrollable list (no click-to-switch tabs). Added an
  in-page ordering flow: each product card has a size/variant selector and a
  quantity stepper; selections build a running order shown in a floating
  cart panel (bottom-right). Every "Order on WhatsApp" button across the
  site (header, hero, how-to-order, contact, and the cart panel) now sends
  an itemised message listing every selected product, variant, and quantity;
  if nothing is selected, it falls back to the original generic order
  message. Updated How to Order steps and FAQ copy to describe the new flow.
- 1.0.1 (2026-08-07): Replaced placeholder SVG art with real brand assets —
  favicon.png (site favicon + apple-touch-icon), logo-mark.png (header/footer
  logo, icon-only), logo-full.jpg (circular wordmark emblem, added to About
  section), and hero.jpg (real hero photograph replacing the illustrated
  flat-lay). Category icons remain placeholder SVGs pending category photos.
- 1.0.0 (2026-08-07): Initial build — Home, Products (5 categories, 12 products),
  About, How to Order, Storage & Usage Notes, FAQ, Contact, Footer with policy
  placeholders (Privacy, Terms, Shipping, Refund), custom SVG illustrations,
  WhatsApp ordering integration.
